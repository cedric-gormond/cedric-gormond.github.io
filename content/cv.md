Senior Data Scientist / ML Engineer with 5 years of experience designing and shipping production ML systems across audio/speech, computer vision, and predictive modeling. Hands-on across the full stack — from model architecture and training to containerized deployment, monitoring, and MLOps on Azure ML. Experienced building LLM-based pipelines and Generative AI applications, including RAG systems and speech-to-text pipelines.

## Experience

### Senior Data Scientist
*[Ad Scientiam](https://www.adscientiam.com) · Paris · Feb 2023 – Present*

- Led end-to-end delivery of production ML systems in audio/speech, computer vision, and predictive modeling, from prototyping to Azure ML deployment.
- Packaged and deployed ML models as Dockerized services on Azure ML, with automated monitoring, logging, and batch inference workflows.
- Built a deep learning system for sound event detection in PyTorch — **92% precision**, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines.
- Designed GPU-accelerated pre-processing pipelines in TensorFlow, processing **7+ hours of audio per minute** on GPU.
- Fine-tuned Vision Transformer (ViT) models for face and body landmarking in video streams.
- Developed a speech-to-text pipeline for automatic scoring of motor speech disorders using a fine-tuned LLM with WER-based evaluation.
- Structured experimentation with MLflow for tracking, versioning, and reproducibility.
- Led a Proof-of-Concept on smartphone-based digital biomarkers for motor function assessment, published in [*Neurology* (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000204969).
